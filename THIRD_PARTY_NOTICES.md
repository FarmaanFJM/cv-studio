## Technology logos

- **devicon** (MIT) — multicolor original logos in `src/renderer/brandIcons.generated.ts`, generated by `npm run gen:icons`. https://github.com/devicons/devicon
- **simple-icons** (CC0-1.0) — single-color logo paths and brand colors in `src/renderer/techIcons.tsx`. https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
- Java and Azure monochrome glyphs are simplified versions embedded in `techIcons.tsx`.
- The generic icons (database, backend, REST API, …) and UI icons are original line drawings (Feather-style).

All logos and brand names are trademarks of their respective owners. They are used only to identify the named technology, without implying any endorsement. Logos are shown in their original colors only where an established multicolor asset (or a published brand color) exists; no colors are invented, and low-contrast cases fall back to monochrome.
